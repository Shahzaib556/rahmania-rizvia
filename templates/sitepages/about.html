About Jamia Rahmania Rizvia Sohawa

Our Story

Jamia Rahmania Rizvia Sohawa was founded in {{ site_settings.established_year }} by {{ site_settings.founder_name }}. Located in Sohawa, District Jhelum, the Jamia serves the community with Quran and dini education, and now keeps its records digitally so that students, teachers and parents always know where things stand.

Our Mission

To teach the Quran and the Islamic sciences with sincerity and discipline, alongside the skills our students need — supported by transparent, digital administration.

Our Values
  • Quran, Tajweed and dini knowledge for every student
  • Adab, discipline, honesty and respect
  • Transparent communication with parents
  • Continuous improvement through technology
